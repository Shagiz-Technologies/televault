# Google Play store listing copy

## App name

TeleVault

## Short description

Back up photos and videos to private Telegram channels you control.

## Full description

TeleVault helps you organize and back up selected photos and videos using private Telegram channels connected to your own Telegram account.

Create separate buckets for different albums or purposes, choose which accessible media to back up, monitor upload status, and retain encrypted metadata snapshots for recovery. Android 14+ selected-media access is supported, so you can limit TeleVault to specific photos and videos.

For sensitive items, TeleVault includes a Vault flow with authenticated client-side encryption and a separate Recovery Key. Normal non-vault uploads are not client-side end-to-end encrypted by TeleVault before they are sent to Telegram.

Important boundaries:

- TeleVault is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram.
- Telegram and TDLib process account authorization, private channels, messages, and uploaded files.
- Telegram account limits, availability, retention, and policies apply.
- Android may delay or stop background work because of network, battery, or device restrictions.
- Logging out or uninstalling TeleVault does not automatically delete remote Telegram channels or uploaded messages.
- Keep an independent backup of important files and retain your Vault Recovery Key.

TeleVault does not include advertising SDKs or an external analytics SDK in the reviewed production implementation.

## Prohibited or misleading claims

Do not use the following without precise qualification and current evidence:

- unlimited storage;
- total privacy;
- end-to-end encryption for every upload;
- instant or guaranteed background backup;
- zero-cost storage guaranteed forever;
- Telegram cannot access uploaded content;
- logout or uninstall deletes remote Telegram data.
